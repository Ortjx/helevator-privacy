Helevator Idle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Helevator Idle (the "Game", package com.helevator.idle) collects, how it is used, and your choices. The Game is developed by MikeLeiv (the "Developer", "we").

1. Information we collect

  • Anonymous player ID. When you first play, the Game creates an anonymous account through Google Firebase Authentication. It is a random identifier and is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Game data. Progress and scores (for example highest round, power and best WAVES result) and the display name you choose may be stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, servers in the EU) to provide leaderboards and to save your progress.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can deliver it.
  • Advertising. The Game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Usage analytics. The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played and to measure our advertising campaigns (for example app opens, rounds reached, bosses defeated, rewarded ads watched and in-app purchases). Analytics uses an app instance ID and may use your advertising ID, and collects device information such as model, OS version and approximate (country-level) location.
  • Google Play Games. If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games player ID and display name with the Game so it can give the sign-in gift and post your highest round to the Google Play Games leaderboard, where other players can see your Play Games name and score. This is handled under Google's privacy policy; you can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games settings.
  • Diagnostics. Google services used by the Game may collect basic crash and performance data.

Your save data is also stored locally on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game, save progress and deliver purchases.
  • To show leaderboards.
  • To show rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
  • To fix bugs and improve the Game.
  • To understand how players use the Game and to measure and improve our ads for the Game on Google Ads.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase)
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity (game engine)

4. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown and before your data is used for advertising measurement.
  • You can request deletion of your online game data at any time by emailing us (see below). Uninstalling the Game removes local data from your device.
  • If you live in the EU/EEA you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

5. Data retention

Online game data is kept while your anonymous account is active and is deleted on request or after 24 months of inactivity.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Google's security controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
